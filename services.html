Services

Occasions we style

You choose the celebration. We prepare the room. Every visit starts with the date, the space, and the feeling you want your guests to walk into.

Occasion list

Birthday

We shape one room around the person being celebrated: a clear place for them, space for the people gathered, and styling that feels joyful and composed.

Anniversary

A softer, quieter room, arranged for conversation. The styling stays personal and uncluttered.

Baby shower

Seating that is easy to use, a gentle feel, and surfaces kept clear so the room stays comfortable for everyone.

Housewarming

Styling that welcomes people into a new home, with room to move and a clear sense of where the gathering begins.

Festival

Seasonal flowers, light, and a table or corner that marks the day, while the rest of the home still feels like yours.

Surprise celebration

We prepare the room in advance and keep the setup discreet, so the moment can happen when you are ready.

Other special event

Tell us what the day is for. We will suggest a way to style the space that fits the occasion and the room you have.
