About

Your home, ready for the moment.

Unique Home Edit was created with a simple idea — your home can feel completely different without changing the home itself.

The idea

We work with the space you already have. The furniture stays. The feeling of the room changes for the season, the occasion, and the mood you want.

We focus on thoughtful details rather than excessive decoration, and we create warm, elegant, celebration-ready spaces. The transformation is temporary and designed around the moment.

Who it is for

Hosts marking a festival, a birthday, an anniversary, a housewarming, or any gathering that deserves a considered room.

How we work in the home

We agree on a look before we arrive. Then we visit, style the space, and leave it ready for your guests. The room should still feel like your home.
